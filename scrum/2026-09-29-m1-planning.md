# M1 Planning Meeting Minutes

**Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Team:** ByteBites  
**Topic:** M1 requirements, task breakdown, and data flow

## Discussion

### M1 task breakdown

The team broke down the M1 work into Epics, User Stories (US), and Tasks.
Epics group the main areas of work, user stories describe the needs of the
system's users, and tasks identify the concrete work needed to implement
the stories.

### Role requirements

The team discussed the functionality needed by different roles, including
customers and restaurant managers, and used these needs to guide the M1
task breakdown.

### Data flow

The team discussed how data moves through the system and drew a Data Flow
Diagram (DFD) to illustrate the relationships between external roles,
system processes, and data stores.

## Decisions

- Organize M1 work into Epics, User Stories, and Tasks.
- Allow team members to choose and complete tasks based on their interests
  and availability.
- Use the role requirements and DFD to guide implementation.
